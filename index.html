How it works

Three steps. About a penny.

No gallery to apply to, no account to rent. Your wallet does it.

  1. Make it

    With any tool you like: an AI image app, a music generator, your phone camera, your own hands.

  2. Mint it in bWalletX

    Open Mint (bMint), pick what you made, add the file and a title. You see the network cost before you sign. Small files cost about a penny.

  3. It’s in your vault

    It’s written to Bitcoin in your name with a timestamp anyone can check, and it shows up in your wallet as yours.

Get bWalletXHow it works

The small print: minting writes a timestamp and your signature to Bitcoin. It proves a file existed at that time and was published by your key. It is not a copyright registration and not a legal guarantee of ownership or originality. Only mint work you have the right to publish. Network costs vary with file size. The hero video is a showreel of real work by VexVoid (including a capture of the live VexVoid AV show), Ninja Punk Girls, NPGX, bMovies creators, bRacer gameplay and bWalletX’s own open-source code, set to VexVoid’s “Shattered Circuits”; its seals and “minted” chips are illustrations, not real mints (credits, vertical cut).
