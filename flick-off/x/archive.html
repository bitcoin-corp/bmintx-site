Your bWalletX locks every post with its own key before anything is written, oldest first. Your wallet asks twice for the whole import: once to lock your archive with your keys, once to fill its pot. Not once per post.

The archive pot

Your archive pays for itself from its own pot, so its transactions never touch your wallet’s everyday addresses. The pot’s key comes from your 12 words, so it’s always yours.

Needs –. Anything left over stays in the pot for later, or goes back to you.

Filling it from your bWalletX links the pot to your wallet once, in that one payment. Sending from somewhere else (an exchange, another wallet) links nothing.
