“Fine, but why bChatX?”

You’re not moving in with us. You’re moving into your own place.

The fair question: why trade one platform for another? You don’t. What you flick goes on Bitcoin, not on our servers. bChatX is just one window onto it.

It’s on Bitcoin, not on us

Every post is written to the Bitcoin ledger, encrypted with your wallet keys. Only your 12 words can open it. We couldn’t delete it or read it if we wanted to.

Any app can read it

Posts use the open Bitcoin Schema format, so other Bitcoin apps can show them too, not just bChatX. If we vanish tomorrow, your posts don’t.

A zip is a dead file

Your archive sitting in Downloads does nothing. Make the posts you choose public on bChatX and they’re a living timeline again: people can follow, reply, tip and share.

Proof it’s yours

Each post keeps its original date and link, sealed by your wallet: a permanent record that you said it first, which you can prove whenever you choose, without showing the rest.
