Questions

Before you flick it.

Does this delete my X account?

No. Your X account stays exactly as it is. This makes a copy you own, on Bitcoin and on bChatX. Whether you keep posting on X is up to you.

Why does fetching cost more than the archive?

X charges per post read through its API. Your archive is free to download from X, so that route skips X’s fee and you only pay to mint.

What does minting cost?

Each post is written to Bitcoin (BSV) in your name. A text post is a fraction of a penny; photos and videos cost more because they’re bigger. The estimate shows the live network price, with no markup hidden in it.

Who owns the minted posts?

You do. They’re minted to your own bWalletX, signed by you, and sit in your vault next to everything else you’ve minted.

Is my archive uploaded when I drop it?

No. It’s read in your browser to count what’s inside. Nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to switch.
